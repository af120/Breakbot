Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you make a reservation, we collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, and any special requests or occasion details you provide. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Process and manage your dining reservations
  • Communicate with you regarding your booking (e.g., confirmations, cancellations)
  • Accommodate special dietary requirements
  • Send marketing communications if you have explicitly opted in via our newsletter

3. Data Retention

Reservation data is kept securely in our database to manage current and historical bookings. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe.

4. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, modification of, or deletion of your personal data. Please contact us at the email provided on our homepage.
