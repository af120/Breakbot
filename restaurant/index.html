Our Story

Crafted with passion,
served with soul

Ember & Oak was born from a simple belief: that dining should be an experience that engages all the senses. Our chef-driven kitchen sources the finest seasonal ingredients from local farms and artisan producers, transforming them into dishes that tell a story on every plate.

Nestled in the heart of the city, our space blends exposed brick warmth with contemporary elegance — an intimate setting where every evening feels like an occasion.
